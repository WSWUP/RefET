Where the water goes, hour by hour

Mean hourly ETr for every hour in the record — 8,758 hourly solutions of the ASCE equation. The bright band is the sun, running 06:00–19:00 at its widest and 09:00–17:00 at its narrowest. The faint vertical streaks reaching into the small hours are real, not noise: the hourly ASCE equation returns non-zero ET overnight whenever wind and dry air persist, and dips slightly negative on calm, humid nights.
